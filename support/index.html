常见问题

提醒没有显示

请检查 iOS 设置中是否允许 HowLong 发送通知，并确认 App 内的截止提醒开关和该事项的提醒规则已开启。

删除的事项在哪里？

普通删除会将事项移入归档；你可以在“归档与历史”中恢复。选择“永久删除”后，事项及其附件会从设备中移除。

识别出的日期不准确

照片识别用于辅助录入。保存前请核对日期；如果格式含糊，应用会让你确认后再添加。

Frequently Asked Questions

Why didn’t I receive a reminder?

Check that iOS allows notifications for HowLong, and that deadline reminders and the item’s reminder rules are enabled in the app.

Where are deleted items?

Items removed normally are moved to Archive and can be restored from Archive & History. Permanently deleted items and their attachments are removed from the device.

Why is a recognized date incorrect?

Photo recognition assists with entry. Review the date before saving; when a numeric date is ambiguous, the app asks you to confirm it.
