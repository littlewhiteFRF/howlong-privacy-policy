Purchases and third-party services

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store; the developer does not receive your full payment-card details. iCloud sync is provided by Apple CloudKit. The current app does not use advertising tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Deletion and retention

Archived items remain in the app. You can choose “Permanently Delete” in the archive to remove an item and its attachments from your device. When automatic sync is enabled, a later successful upload replaces the previous iCloud backup with the current one. Turning off automatic sync does not delete an existing iCloud backup.
