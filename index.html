关于本政策

本政策说明“还有：到期提醒”（HowLong）如何处理你在应用中保存的信息。开发者：荣福 方。

应用处理哪些信息

  • 你输入或保存的事项标题、日期、分类、备注、提醒规则、归档、每日必做内容、完成记录和相关附件。
  • 你选择或拍摄的图片和文件。图片文字识别由 Apple Vision 在设备上完成；如果你使用 iCloud 备份，备份也可能包含这些附件。
  • 保存在设备上的应用偏好和 Premium 权益状态，用于提供相应功能。

信息保存和 iCloud 同步

事项数据默认保存在你的设备上。应用没有单独的账号系统，也没有用于保存事项内容的开发者服务器。

Premium 用户可以手动上传或恢复 iCloud 备份，也可以开启自动同步。上传时，应用会把当前备份写入与你的 Apple 账户关联的 CloudKit 私有数据库，供你在自己的设备间恢复。备份可能包含事项、分类、提醒规则、归档、每日必做和完成记录，以及相关图片或文件附件。该数据库默认仅由你访问，数据计入你的 iCloud 储存空间；Apple 对 iCloud 服务的处理受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

通知、导出和分享

截止日期和每日提醒由 iOS 本地通知功能安排。应用不会通过开发者运营的推送服务器发送这些提醒。

当你选择导出备份、分享事项或分享反馈模板时，内容会交给你在系统分享面板中选择的目标。接收方如何处理这些内容，由接收方的政策决定。

购买和第三方服务

App 内购买由 Apple App Store 处理；开发者不会收到你的完整付款卡信息。iCloud 同步由 Apple CloudKit 提供。当前应用不使用广告跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。

删除和保留

归档的事项仍保留在应用中；你可以在归档中选择“永久删除”以从设备中移除事项及其附件。开启自动同步后，后续成功上传会用当前备份替换 iCloud 中的上一份备份。关闭自动同步不会自动删除已有的 iCloud 备份。你可以在 Apple 账户的 iCloud 储存空间管理中查看并删除本应用的 iCloud 数据；也可以在系统设置中关闭本应用的 iCloud 使用权限。菜单名称可能因系统版本而异，参见 Apple 关于管理第三方 App iCloud 数据的说明。Apple 对其服务中的数据保留和删除负责。

本政策的更新

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，开发者会更新本页面的内容和日期。

About this policy

This policy explains how 还有：到期提醒 (HowLong) handles information you save in the app. Developer: Rongfu Fang (荣福 方).

Information the app handles

  • Items you create, including titles, dates, categories, notes, reminder rules, archives, daily tasks, completion records, and related attachments.
  • Images and files you choose or capture. Apple Vision recognizes image text on your device. If you use iCloud backup, the backup may also include these attachments.
  • App preferences and Premium entitlement status stored on your device, to provide the corresponding features.

Storage and iCloud sync

Item data is stored on your device by default. The app does not have a separate account system or a developer-operated server for storing item content.

Premium users can manually upload or restore an iCloud backup, and can enable automatic sync. When uploaded, the app writes the current backup to the CloudKit private database associated with your Apple Account so you can restore it on your own devices. A backup may include items, categories, reminder rules, archives, daily tasks and completion records, and related images or file attachments. By default, only you can access this database, and its data counts toward your iCloud storage. Apple’s handling of iCloud services is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Notifications, export, and sharing

Expiry and daily reminders are scheduled with iOS local notifications. The app does not send these reminders through a developer-operated push server.

When you export a backup or share an item or feedback template, the content is handed to the destination you choose in the system share sheet. The recipient’s own policies govern its handling of that content.

Purchases and third-party services

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store; the developer does not receive your full payment-card details. iCloud sync is provided by Apple CloudKit. The current app does not use advertising tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Deletion and retention

Archived items remain in the app. You can choose “Permanently Delete” in the archive to remove an item and its attachments from your device. When automatic sync is enabled, a later successful upload replaces the previous iCloud backup with the current one. Turning off automatic sync does not delete an existing iCloud backup. You can review and delete this app’s iCloud data in your Apple Account’s iCloud storage management, or turn off this app’s iCloud access in system settings. Menu names may vary by system version. See Apple’s instructions for managing third-party app iCloud data. Apple is responsible for retention and deletion of data in its services.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, the developer will update this page and its revision date.

Privacy contact

For questions about this policy or personal information, contact: rolf1120802408@gmail.com
